Use the `marketing-campaign` skill if it is available. Otherwise:

1. Ask for what is missing: the product, the goal (with a number and a date), the audience, the budget, and the channels already working.
2. Write the plan: audience and the problem it has, positioning in one sentence, two or three key messages, channels with why each fits, a week-by-week calendar, the budget split, and how success is measured.
3. Point out the riskiest assumption and a cheap way to test it first.
