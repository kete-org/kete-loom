# Page and funnel audit checklist

Score each line OK / Problem / Not checked, and attach evidence for every Problem.

## Arrival

- [ ] Headline repeats the promise of the traffic source (ad, email, search snippet)
- [ ] Offer, price and wording match what was promised
- [ ] Page loads fast on a mid-range phone over mobile data
- [ ] Nothing shifts or covers the content in the first seconds (cookie banner, pop-up)

## Clarity

- [ ] A stranger can say what it is, who it's for and what to do next in five seconds
- [ ] One primary call to action; secondary ones visually quieter
- [ ] Call to action says what happens ("Start free trial", not "Submit")
- [ ] Benefits before features; jargon replaced with the customer's words

## Friction

- [ ] Every form field is needed now; the rest asked later
- [ ] Input types and autocomplete set correctly (email, tel, postal code)
- [ ] Errors are shown inline, explain the fix, and keep what was typed
- [ ] No forced account creation before value where guest flow is possible
- [ ] Total cost, fees, tax and shipping visible before the last step
- [ ] Works with keyboard, screen reader, and zoom (accessibility problems are conversion problems)

## Trust

- [ ] Proof near the decision point: named customers, reviews, ratings with counts
- [ ] Security, refund, cancellation and contact information easy to find
- [ ] No unverifiable claims, fake countdowns or invented scarcity

## Pricing page

- [ ] Plans comparable at a glance; differences stated, not hidden in footnotes
- [ ] Recommended plan clear and justified
- [ ] Monthly versus annual cost shown in the same unit
- [ ] Trial terms: length, card needed or not, what happens at the end
- [ ] FAQ answers the top objections from sales and support

## Evidence sources

Analytics funnel and segment data, session recordings and heatmaps (with consent), on-page polls ("What's stopping you today?"), support and sales objections, usability tests with five users.
