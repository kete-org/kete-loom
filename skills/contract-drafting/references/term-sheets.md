# Term sheet fields and pre-review checklists

Read this when collecting inputs for a draft (section 0) or running the agreement-type checks (section 4). Required fields are marked `*`. Ask for every missing required field.

## Common to all agreements

- `*` Parties: full legal name, entity type, jurisdiction of incorporation, registered address, company number where used.
- `*` Effective date, and signatories (name, title).
- `*` Governing law and dispute forum (courts or arbitration, seat, rules).
- Notices: addresses and email for formal notice.
- Template name and version used.

## NDA

- `*` Mutual or one-way; who discloses.
- `*` Purpose (the permitted use of the information).
- `*` Term of the agreement and how long confidentiality obligations survive; any trade secrets carve-out to last longer.
- Permitted disclosees (employees, affiliates, advisers, financing sources).
- Exclusions: public, already known, independently developed, received from a third party lawfully; compelled disclosure process.
- Return or destruction on request, with a backup-copy carve-out.
- Non-solicitation or standstill (high risk; often belongs in a separate agreement).

## MSA

- `*` Services or products in scope; how orders or SOWs are created.
- `*` Fees, currency, invoicing schedule, payment terms, late payment, price changes, taxes.
- `*` Liability cap (amount or formula, for example fees paid in the prior 12 months) and exclusions from the cap.
- `*` Term, renewal (auto-renew and notice), termination for cause and for convenience, effects of termination.
- Indemnities: who indemnifies whom, for what (IP infringement, data breach, third-party claims), procedure.
- Warranties and disclaimers; service levels and credits as a schedule.
- IP: background IP stays with its owner; ownership of deliverables; licences granted.
- Data protection: whether a DPA is needed; security schedule.
- Insurance types and minimums.
- Subcontracting, assignment and change of control.
- Order of precedence between MSA, SOW, schedules and purchase orders.

## SOW

- `*` Reference to the governing MSA and its date.
- `*` Deliverables with descriptions; milestones and dates.
- `*` Acceptance criteria, acceptance period, and what happens on rejection.
- `*` Fees per milestone or rate card, expenses policy.
- Assumptions and customer dependencies; change-request process.
- Named contacts and governance meetings.
- Checks: the SOW must not change liability, IP or payment terms set in the MSA unless the MSA allows it, and then the change is a logged deviation.

## DPA

- `*` Roles: controller and processor (or processor and sub-processor).
- `*` Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing.
- `*` Types of personal data, special categories if any, categories of data subjects.
- `*` Security measures (usually an annex).
- `*` Sub-processors: list, approval method (specific or general with notice and objection).
- Breach notice period from processor to controller.
- Assistance with data subject requests, security, impact assessments and prior consultation.
- Deletion or return at end of services.
- Audits and information rights.
- International transfers: destinations and mechanism (EU SCCs 2021 module, UK IDTA or addendum). Route to a privacy lawyer.

Check each DPA against the processor terms of GDPR Article 28(3) and the law of the user's jurisdiction; other data protection laws have their own processor contract requirements.

## Final pre-review checks (all types)

- Every placeholder filled or logged as a deviation; no `[ ]` left unexplained.
- Defined terms defined once and used consistently; cross-references valid.
- Dates, amounts and currencies match the term sheet exactly (amounts in words and figures agree if both are used).
- Signature blocks match the party names.
- Draft marking present on every page or in the header.
