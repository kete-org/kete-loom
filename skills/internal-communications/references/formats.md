# Internal communication templates

Use the template for the format chosen in step 0. Fill every placeholder from gathered facts; leave `[confirm: ...]` where a fact is missing.

## 3P update (weekly)

```
<Team or name>, week of <date>. Overall: <On track | At risk | Off track>

Progress
- <Shipped or finished item, with link and impact>
- ...

Plans (next week)
- <Item, owner, target date>
- ...

Problems
- <Blocker or risk, impact, what is being done, help needed from whom>
```

Rules: 2-5 bullets per section. Problems may be empty; say "None this week" rather than omitting the heading.

## Project status report

```
<Project> status, <date>
Status: <On track | At risk | Off track>  (last report: <status>)
Target: <milestone> on <date>  (changed from <date>, because <reason>, if moved)

Summary: <two sentences: where we are and what matters most now>

Decisions needed
- <Decision, options, recommended option, who decides, by when>

Milestones
| Milestone | Owner | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

Risks and issues
| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Metrics: <key metric: current vs previous vs target>
Links: <tracker board>, <plan doc>
```

## Leadership brief

```
Subject: <Topic>: <status or decision needed in under 10 words>

Bottom line: <one sentence>
Ask: <decision or support needed, by when> (or "No action needed")

What changed since last update
- <point>
- <point>

Risks: <one or two, with mitigation>
Detail: <link>
```

Keep under 250 words. No background the reader already has.

## Newsletter

```
<Title>, <issue or date>

In this issue: <three to five one-line teasers>

## Headline story
<What happened, why it matters to the reader, what's next. 100-200 words.>

## Wins
- <Team>: <win, with a number if possible>

## Coming up
- <Date>: <event or deadline>

## People
<Welcomes, moves, anniversaries, only with the people's agreement>

## Questions or ideas
<Where to send them>
```

## Internal FAQ

```
<Topic> FAQ, last updated <date>, owner <name or team>

Q: <Question in the reader's words>
A: <Direct answer in the first sentence. Then detail or a link.>
```

Order by how many people ask, not by topic logic. Include the awkward questions people will ask anyway. Mark answers that are not final as `Not decided yet; expected by <date>`.

## Change announcement

```
Subject: <What is changing> from <date>

What is changing: <one or two sentences>
When: <date and time, with time zone>
Who it affects: <teams, roles, locations>
Why: <the reason, plainly, in two or three sentences>
What you need to do: <action, deadline>  (or "Nothing; this happens automatically")
Questions: <FAQ link, channel, named contact>
```
