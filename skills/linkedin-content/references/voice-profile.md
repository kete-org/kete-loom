# Voice profile template

Save as `~/.kete-loom/linkedin/voice.md` (a person) or a project file such as `docs/linkedin-voice.md` (a company page). The user owns this file: propose changes, never apply them silently. Fill it from the user's own posts; mark anything inferred as `(guess)`.

`post-check.js --voice <file>` reads the "Words I would never use" line, so keep it as one comma-separated line.

```markdown
# LinkedIn voice: <name or page>

## Who
- What I do, in one sentence:
- Who I write for (specific: "finance leads at 50-500 person SaaS companies", not "professionals"):
- What I want them to do after reading (hire, buy, follow, apply, think differently about X):

## How I sound
- Three posts that sound most like me: <links or pasted text>
- Words I actually use:
- **Words I would never use:** synergy, thought leader, game-changer
- Sentence length: short / mixed / long
- Contractions: yes / no
- Emoji: never / rarely / freely
- Person: I / we
- Spelling: British / American

## Positions
Three to five things I believe that some of my audience doesn't. Posts come from here.
1.
2.
3.

## Proof I can use
Real numbers, outcomes and stories I'm happy to put my name on. Drafts never invent these; if this is empty they come back with {{your number}}.
-

## Off limits
- Topics I don't post about:
- Clients, employers or numbers I can't name:
- Claims I'm not allowed to make (regulated, NDA, employer policy):
- Needs approval from someone else first (legal, comms, a client):

## Practice
- Links: in the post / in the first comment
- Hashtags: none / up to three
- Posting rhythm I can actually keep:
- Audience time zone:
```
