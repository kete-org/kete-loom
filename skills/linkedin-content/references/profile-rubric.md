# Profile rubric (100 points)

Score honestly from what the user pastes or screenshots; first-pass profiles commonly land below 50. Show the score per item with one line of evidence, then rewrite in the order of points lost.

| Item | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | 12 | says who the user helps and what changes for them, with one piece of proof; not just a job title |
| About, first lines | 10 | the lines visible before "see more" name the reader's problem; no "passionate about", no third person |
| About, body | 10 | written to one reader: their problem, what the user does, one real proof point, what to do next |
| Current role | 10 | one line of scope, then two or three outcomes with numbers the user can share; not duties |
| Earlier experience | 8 | the last two roles detailed; older roles compressed |
| Featured | 8 | up to three current items: best post, a proof asset, a way to get in touch |
| Photo | 6 | recent, face clearly visible, plain background |
| Banner | 6 | one line of positioning or a way to reach the user; not a default or stock image |
| Activity | 8 | posts or comments in the last month that match the headline's promise |
| Recommendations | 8 | recent, specific ones from people the target reader would trust |
| Skills | 6 | the top skills match what the user wants to be hired or found for |
| Custom URL and contact | 8 | a clean profile URL; contact or booking link the user wants public |

Rewrite rules: the user's real titles, dates and numbers only; mark anything needing a number with `{{your number}}`; never claim employers, clients, certifications or results the user didn't give. Keep the voice profile's tone.

Output:

```
PROFILE SCORE  <n>/100
<item>  <got>/<max>  <one line of evidence>
...
FIX FIRST  (most points recoverable)
1. Headline: <rewrite option A> | <option B>
2. ...
Needs from you: <numbers, links, permissions>
```
