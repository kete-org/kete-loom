# Machine-prose sweep

Read this during step 7 of the editing pass. Search the draft for each pattern; for every hit, apply the fix or leave it with a reason. Readers who spot two or three of these stop trusting the rest of the piece.

## P0: always fix

| Pattern | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot residue | "Certainly! Here's a draft...", "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if you'd like changes" | Delete. The article starts at the hook and ends at the close. |
| Cutoff or self-reference disclaimers | "As of my last update...", "As an AI, I can't..." | Delete, then check the fact the disclaimer was hedging and date it properly ("as of March 2026"). |
| Unattributed authority | "Experts agree...", "Studies show...", "Many believe..." | Name the expert or study and link it, or cut the claim. Mark `[CHECK: ...]` if the author must supply it. |
| Inflated stakes | A minor release called "a watershed moment", a routine hire "a bold new chapter" | Say what happened and why it matters to this reader, in proportion. |

## P1: fix unless the author's voice needs it

| Pattern | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Stock vocabulary | delve, leverage, robust, seamless, tapestry, landscape, realm, unlock, elevate, harness, pivotal, navigate (figuratively), game-changer | Use the plain verb or noun: look at, use, reliable, smooth. If nothing plain fits, the sentence may be empty; cut it. |
| Scene-setting openers | "In the rapidly evolving world of...", "In today's digital age..." | Open with the hook from step 4. |
| Signpost transitions | "Let's dive in", "Let's explore", "Without further ado" | Delete; the subheading already signals the turn. |
| Synonym cycling | "the tool... the platform... the solution... the offering" for one product | Pick one term and keep it. Variety here reads as several different things. |
| Hedge stacks | "could potentially", "may possibly help to", "it might be argued that" | One hedge at most, and only where the evidence is uncertain. |
| Tidy triplets | "fast, flexible, and future-proof" on every claim | Keep the one item you can support; drop the rhythm filler. |
| "Not X, but Y" reflex | "It's not just a tool, it's a mindset" | State Y directly. |
| Bold overuse | Several bolded phrases per paragraph | Bold only terms a skimmer must not miss, a few per article. |
| Bare noun-phrase bullets | "- Scalability\n- Security\n- Speed" in the middle of an argument | Turn them into sentences that make a claim, or into prose. |
| Summary close | "In conclusion, ..." restating each section | End with the takeaway, next step or call to action (step 4). |

## Rules for the sweep

- Fix the sentence, not just the word: swapping "leverage" for "use" in an empty sentence leaves an empty sentence.
- Don't introduce new tells while fixing (replacing "delve" with "dig deep into").
- When editing someone's own draft, list P1 changes for them rather than silently rewriting their style.
