# Interview guide template

Copy and fill in. Keep it to one page; the interviewer should glance at it, not read it out.

## Header

- Outcome this research serves:
- Segment and screener (who qualifies, who does not):
- Interviewer / note-taker:
- Consent: recording yes/no, notes anonymised before sharing

## Opening (5 min)

- What is your role, and what does a normal week look like?
- Where does [problem area] fit into that week?

## The last time (20 to 30 min)

- Tell me about the last time you [had to do the job / hit the problem].
  - What set it off?
  - What did you do first? Then what?
  - What tools, files or people were involved?
  - What was hard, slow or annoying? What did it cost (time, money, reputation)?
  - How did it end? Were you happy with the result?
- Have you tried other ways to handle it? Why did you stop?
- Is there a time it went badly? Walk me through that one.

Follow-up probes: "Can you show me?", "Why was that?", "What happened next?", "What did you do instead?"

## Close (5 min)

- What should I have asked that I didn't?
- Who else should I talk to?
- May we follow up?

## Avoid

| Instead of | Ask |
|---|---|
| Would you use a tool that ...? | How do you handle that today? |
| How much would you pay? | What do you spend on it now (time, tools, people)? |
| Don't you hate it when ...? | Tell me about the last time that happened. |
| Do you like this idea? | (Don't show the idea in a discovery interview.) |
