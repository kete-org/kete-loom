# Incoterms 2020 quick reference

Published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC). Eleven rules. This is a summary for building systems; the ICC text governs. The parties may still contract a different arrangement, so read the sales contract.

## Rules for any mode of transport

| Rule | Seller delivers / risk passes | Main carriage paid by | Export clearance | Import clearance and duties |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXW Ex Works | Goods at seller's premises, not loaded | Buyer | Buyer | Buyer |
| FCA Free Carrier | Handed to buyer's carrier at named place (loaded if at seller's premises) | Buyer | Seller | Buyer |
| CPT Carriage Paid To | Handed to the first carrier | Seller (to named destination) | Seller | Buyer |
| CIP Carriage and Insurance Paid To | Handed to the first carrier | Seller, plus insurance | Seller | Buyer |
| DAP Delivered at Place | At named destination, ready for unloading | Seller | Seller | Buyer |
| DPU Delivered at Place Unloaded | At named destination, unloaded | Seller | Seller | Buyer |
| DDP Delivered Duty Paid | At named destination, cleared for import | Seller | Seller | Seller |

## Rules for sea and inland waterway only

| Rule | Seller delivers / risk passes | Main carriage paid by | Export | Import |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FAS Free Alongside Ship | Alongside the vessel at port of shipment | Buyer | Seller | Buyer |
| FOB Free On Board | On board the vessel at port of shipment | Buyer | Seller | Buyer |
| CFR Cost and Freight | On board the vessel at port of shipment | Seller (to destination port) | Seller | Buyer |
| CIF Cost, Insurance and Freight | On board the vessel at port of shipment | Seller, plus insurance | Seller | Buyer |

## Points that cause mistakes

- In C rules (CPT, CIP, CFR, CIF) the seller pays carriage to the destination, but **risk passes at shipment**. Damage in transit is the buyer's risk.
- Insurance: CIP requires cover at the level of Institute Cargo Clauses (A) (all risks, subject to exclusions); CIF requires the minimum, Clauses (C). Both at least 110% of the contract value. Parties may agree otherwise.
- DPU replaced DAT in 2020 and can be any place, not only a terminal.
- FCA in 2020 lets the parties agree that the buyer's carrier issues an on-board bill of lading to the seller, which matters for letters of credit.
- Under DDP, VAT or GST at import is the seller's cost unless the contract says otherwise; the seller may need a tax registration in the destination.
- Incoterms say nothing about title transfer, payment, or which law applies.

## Choosing a rule (default guidance, confirm with the user)

| Situation | Usually |
|---|---|
| E-commerce to consumers, seller wants a no-surprise delivery | DDP if registered for import taxes in the destination, otherwise DAP and tell the customer duties are due on delivery |
| B2B, buyer has its own forwarder | FCA at seller's premises or a named hub |
| B2B, seller arranges freight | CPT or CIP (container) / CFR or CIF (bulk sea cargo only) |
| Buyer collects and seller cannot do export filings | EXW only if the buyer can legally act as exporter; otherwise FCA |
