# Investor materials: templates

Read this when drafting. Replace every `<...>` with the founder's facts; leave a visible `TODO` where a fact is missing instead of guessing.

## Pitch deck slide prompts

| Slide | Headline pattern | Body | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | `<Company>: <what it does for whom>` | Logo, contact, round | Understandable without speaking |
| Problem | `<Customer> loses <cost> because <cause>` | Quote or data point, current workaround | Specific customer, not "everyone" |
| Solution | `<Product> lets <customer> <outcome>` | Screenshot or 3-step flow | Shows the product |
| Why now | `<Change> makes this possible now` | 1-3 changes with dates | Real change, not hype |
| Traction | `<Metric> grew <x> in <period>` | One chart, 2-3 supporting numbers | Dates, definitions, axes |
| Market | `<N> <customers> x <price> = <size>` | Bottom-up arithmetic, sources | Shows the calculation |
| Business model | `We earn <price> per <unit>` | Pricing, margin, unit economics | Matches traction numbers |
| Competition | `Customers choose us because <reason>` | Matrix or table of alternatives | Includes "do nothing" |
| Go-to-market | `We acquire customers through <channel>` | Current CAC or conversion if known | Evidence, not plans only |
| Team | `<Team> has done <relevant thing>` | Founders, key hires, advisers | Relevance to this problem |
| Ask | `Raising <amount> to reach <milestone>` | Instrument, use of funds, runway | Percentages sum to 100 |

## One-pager

```
# <Company>: <one-line description>
<Website> | <Founder name, email> | Raising <amount> <instrument>

**Problem.** <2 sentences>
**Solution.** <2 sentences>
**Traction.** <best metric with date>; <2 supporting numbers>
**Market.** <bottom-up size, one line of arithmetic>
**Business model.** <how you charge, price>
**Competition.** <alternatives and why customers pick you>
**Team.** <founders, one line each>
**The ask.** <amount>, <use of funds in 3 buckets>, reaching <milestone> with <n> months runway
```

## Review checklist

- [ ] Narrative fits in five sentences and every slide supports one.
- [ ] Each metric is defined and dated, and matches across documents.
- [ ] The ask has an amount, instrument, use of funds and milestone.
- [ ] Market size includes a bottom-up calculation.
- [ ] Competition slide names real alternatives.
- [ ] Projections are labelled as projections.
- [ ] No claim without a source the founder can show.
